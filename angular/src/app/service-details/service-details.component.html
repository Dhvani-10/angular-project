Income Certificate Service

Income Certificate is an official document issued by the Municipal Corporation to certify the annual income of a citizen. It is required for government schemes, scholarships, fee concessions, and welfare benefits.

Required Documents

  • Aadhar Card (Applicant)
  • Address Proof (Electricity Bill / Ration Card)
  • Income Proof (Salary Slip / Self Declaration / Employer Certificate)
  • Passport Size Photograph

Processing Time

5 – 7 Working Days

Eligibility

Applicant must be a resident of Navsari Municipal Corporation area.

Property Tax Management

Property Tax is a mandatory annual tax collected by the Municipal Corporation for maintenance of roads, sanitation, water supply, and other civic facilities.

Required Details

  • Property ID / Assessment Number
  • Owner Name
  • Property Address
  • Mobile Number

Additional Information

Tax can be paid online or at authorized municipal centers. Late payment may include penalty charges.

Water Supply Services

This service allows citizens to apply for new water connections, update billing details, and report water supply issues within Navsari Municipal Corporation area.

Required Documents

  • Aadhar Card
  • Address Proof
  • Property Ownership Proof / Rent Agreement
  • Passport Size Photograph

Services Available

  • New Water Connection
  • Bill Correction
  • Complaint Registration

Building Plan Approval

Building Plan Approval is required before starting any residential or commercial construction within municipal limits. It ensures compliance with safety and urban planning rules.

Required Documents

  • Property Ownership Document
  • Architect Approved Building Plan
  • Land Tax Receipt
  • Identity Proof (Aadhar Card)
  • Site Map / Layout Plan

Approval Process

  • Application Submission
  • Document Verification
  • Site Inspection
  • Final Approval by Authority

Processing Time

10 – 15 Working Days
