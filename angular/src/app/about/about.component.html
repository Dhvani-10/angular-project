WHO WE ARE

About Us

Navsari Municipal Corporation is the governing body responsible for managing the civic infrastructure and public services of Navsari city. It works with a vision to ensure planned urban development, better living standards, and efficient service delivery to all citizens.

Through digital transformation, smart governance initiatives, and citizen-first policies, we aim to build a transparent, responsive, and well-managed city administration system.

Why Choose Us?

✔ Transparent & accountable governance system

✔ Smart city development initiatives

✔ Fast grievance redressal system

✔ Clean & green city projects

✔ Digital & online civic services

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver efficient, transparent, and high-quality civic services that improve daily life for every citizen. We focus on essential services like water supply, sanitation, road maintenance, waste management, and quick grievance resolution.

We continuously work on improving digital services, reducing manual delays, and ensuring accountability in every department for better governance.

👁

Our Vision

Our vision is to transform Navsari into a modern, smart, and sustainable city that provides world-class infrastructure and services to its citizens.

We aim to build a future-ready city with clean environment, digital systems, and active citizen participation in governance.
