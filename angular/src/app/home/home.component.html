City Highlights

Municipal Office

The administrative headquarters of Navsari Municipal Corporation, serving as the central hub for governance, citizen services, urban planning, and public welfare initiatives. It ensures transparency, efficiency, and community-focused development.

Dandi – Historic Salt March Site

Dandi is a historic coastal village near Navsari, globally recognized for the Salt March led by Mahatma Gandhi in 1930. It stands as a symbol of India’s freedom movement and continues to attract visitors for its heritage and scenic shoreline.

Public Service Center

A dedicated center providing essential civic services including documentation assistance, grievance redressal, tax services, and citizen support programs to ensure smooth and accessible municipal operations for all residents.
